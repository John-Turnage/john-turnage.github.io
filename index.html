I am a mathematics PhD candidate at the University of Utah, advised by Akil Narayan and affiliated with the Scientific Computing and Imaging Institute. I expect to complete my PhD in May 2027.

I study how to construct accurate approximations from limited data, using approximation theory and probability. Much of my work concerns learning PDE solution operators from simulations. I develop spaces of approximating operators, sampling procedures for stable recovery, and methods for estimating the component left unresolved by a finite approximation. Related work concerns quadrature, reduced-order models, and inverse problems.
